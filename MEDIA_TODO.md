# Media cabinet checklist

These assets are intentionally not published yet. Review every image for privacy,
permission and factual captions before copying it into `public/`.

## Suggested material

- 8–12 favourite photographs, preferably landscapes, food, street scenes or portraits
  Samuel owns and has permission to publish.
- Wedding-photography examples only where the couple and identifiable guests have
  explicitly agreed to public portfolio use.
- 2–4 King's College London Brass Band / UniBrass images, with the competition year,
  result and photographer credit confirmed.
- A programme, poster or rehearsal image for each production: *Thoroughly Modern
  Millie*, *Tombalek* and *Curtains*. Record Samuel's exact role and production year.
- Home-lab photographs: one full-rack view, one hand-built server, the NAS and a
  Raspberry Pi. Remove visible IP addresses, hostnames, serial numbers, QR codes,
  credentials and private dashboards.
- A screenshot or surviving build of the Visual Basic periodic-table application.
- COVERD screenshots made with synthetic candidates and company data. Obtain approval
  before showing any design-partner name, logo, conversation or result.
- One relaxed headshot and one performance photograph to complement the formal portrait.

## File preparation

- Keep an archival original outside the repository.
- Export web copies as AVIF or WebP at roughly 1,600 px on the long edge.
- Use descriptive lowercase filenames, for example
  `unibrass-kcl-euphonium-2022.webp`.
- Add a one-sentence factual caption, date, photographer credit and useful alt text.
- Do not rely on EXIF; strip location metadata from public exports.
