## Repairs prompted by the review

The original CV invitation led to a disabled button because its sample was already analysed. The opening now says so, starts at the matching results, and explains suggested edits through the actual CV sentences. Prominent evidence/proof labels became experience, requirements and sentence clarity.

The insurance opening previously put two research-contract result blocks and a retired aggregate model before the broker task. I removed that score wall, made comparison of insurers the first purpose, moved the older method comparison behind a disclosure and changed repeated retired blanks into a useful count of completed checks. Missing information remains visible and still blocks pinning.

STUDY-RL originally opened on repository review counts, QA flags and writing provenance. It now starts with the working CliffWalking agent, explains the 100-point cliff penalty and keeps the curriculum available as another workspace. Writing-process credit was removed from the presentation; technical AI learning content remains.

Italian originally began with an unavailable listening exercise without a usable skip. It now starts with a visible multiple-choice question; listening retains an explicit text-exercise fallback. The curriculum/system overview follows practice, and English navigation labels stay visible on phones in bilingual mode.

The scientific/tool demos now identify my contribution in ordinary language, name a first control and explain its result. Scheduling's calendar-delivery diagram follows the scheduling interaction. Molecular copy describes my spectroscopy work directly without group/co-author positioning. Home-lab guidance names the two-step fault/graph action instead of implying that selecting a database alone highlights the failure paths.
