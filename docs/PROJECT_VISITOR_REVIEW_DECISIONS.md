## Decisions from the review

- Reliability, Regularisation and Causal/OPE originally opened with generic method descriptions and vague multi-step instructions. Their purpose now states what I studied or built in ordinary language; Try names a visible control and one useful outcome. The separate synthetic/recorded-result distinctions remain accurate.
- Air-quality Try now gives the concrete £3,500 → £2,500 change. Its result explains RMSE instead of leaving a visitor with an unexplained decimal. The different full-model test evaluation remains separate from the two cross-validation configurations, and prices remain hypothetical.
- Cyber Try now raises the alert threshold from the useful default, revealing a meaningful error trade-off. Lowering the initial 0.20 threshold could not reduce its already-zero missed attacks. No dataset-leakage limitation or withheld detection-performance claim was removed.
- Fraud deferral's technical tail-risk view is labelled Worst losses instead of CVaR ledger; the methods and hard-case calculations remain available. The Causal/OPE result label is Policy estimates instead of Estimator receipt. Coverage shift explains my separate model evaluation instead of calling it an evidence record.
- The selected Challenger policy label was white on a pale blue background in the phone screenshot. A targeted `.policyChoices label.policyActive` repair uses the shared black ink token and retains the existing meaningful selection background. Final phone observation confirmed `rgb(0, 0, 0)` text on `rgb(228, 231, 255)`; desktop screenshot confirmed the same readable selected control.

All six explanations and purposeful actions were checked on desktop and phone. Phone layouts need vertical scrolling through the introduction to the controls; they preserve readable prose, controls and results. Wider plots expose their existing sideways-scroll/keyboard hint instead of shrinking labels into unreadable text. The remaining deduction reflects this reading/scrolling cost and the technical detail in optional method panels, rather than a missing primary task. No universal score is claimed for every possible demo state.
