# Original-image gallery and shared-player cross-review

This is an independent agent review of the source-image agent's 13 galleries and the coordinator's shared image/concept player. It is a qualitative browser review, not a human user study. The reviewer asked what the project does, what each original image shows, how to read it, and how the illustrated sequence differs from a saved result. The 27 product scenes and 15 science/decision scenes have separate cross-reviews by their other authors.

## Reviewed journeys

All 13 gallery routes and all 18 registered source images were opened on the compiled application. Every alternative image was selected, inspected at its original pixel dimensions, and returned to the fitted view. All four manual concept stages were exercised for each gallery route. The same journeys were repeated at desktop 1466×858, phone 390×844 and narrow phone 320×740. The complete final gallery geometry/control matrix uses the compiled preview on port 3211. A final desktop recheck on port 3213 confirms the unchanged shared frame and 44px/60px controls after the unrelated CV/COVERD scene repairs. The narrow fitted image pane is 266px wide; the 390px phone pane is 336px wide. Image proportions stay within integer-pixel rounding of the original ratio, with no distorted plots or deliberate frame crop in the fitted view.

The inspector preserves the image's natural size and confines horizontal/vertical scrolling to its focusable region. Keyboard Right changed the Safety plot's horizontal position; its natural 500×392 pixels remain available inside the 336px pane. The 586px methanol and 599px CO₂/water figures receive the same control. The wide air-quality plot and tall OPE/walk figures also remain fully inspectable. Existing GROWMAT redaction and original source lettering remain unchanged.

Arrow keys switch the Images/Core idea tabs; Play exposes Pause, Pause stops playback, Next advances the selected stage and Start again restores stage one. Stage four leaves Next disabled. The compiled common stage buttons are at least 60px tall and the gallery/player controls at least 44px. Phone stages wrap into two columns without widening the project pane. Captions and example notes remain ordinary readable text below the scene, and the project article scrolls normally when the complete sequence is taller than the phone.

UK English received the complete route matrix. Simplified Chinese CPROT was additionally opened in fitted/full-size views and Core idea; Traditional Chinese thermodynamics was opened in both source figures and its animated concept. The translated methanol caption explains the original K⁻¹ axis typo rather than silently altering the source. The existing four-locale visual gate covers 42×4×4 concept states and every image caption/alt/label; this browser review does not claim a complete four-locale manual matrix.
