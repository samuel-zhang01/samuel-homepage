## Review contract

The initial scene states the task and shows a concrete input or starting state. Advancing a stage changes a visible result and explains why it matters. Screenshots are original app/report views; conceptual examples are identified as examples and do not imply new measured performance. Dense plots need a useful caption and readable detail view.

Image framing preserves aspect ratio and native resolution, without stretching a small image to fill the page. Narrow containers wrap controls and expose full-size inspection through intentional scrolling. Animations require an explicit Play action, provide Pause/manual stages, and suspend when hidden, inactive, offscreen or reduced motion is selected. Reviewer judgments remain scoped to the described journeys, with failed rounds retained.
